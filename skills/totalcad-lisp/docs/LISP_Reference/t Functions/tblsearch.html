tblsearch

(tblsearch table-name entry-id [next-flag])

Locates a specific entry within a designated CAD table structure.

This routine fetches property details for a specified entry identifier from a given table. It outputs a sequence of dotted pairs containing the entry's attributes when provided with valid table and entry identifiers.

When next-flag is non-nil (e.g., specified as T), subsequent tblnext operations will continue sequential table traversal:

(tblsearch "Block" "Chair" T)

(tblnext "BLOCK")

The output format mirrors the structure returned by tblnext function calls.

Examples

Code Result
(tblsearch "layer" "0") ((0 . "LAYER") (2 . "0") (70 . 64) (6 . "Continuous") (62 . 7) (290 . 1) (370 . -3))
(tblsearch "style" "standard") ((0 . "STYLE") (2 . "Standard") (70 . 64) (3 . "txt") (4 . "") (40 . 0.000000) (41 . 1.00000) (42 . 2.50000) (50 . 0.000000) (71 . 0))
